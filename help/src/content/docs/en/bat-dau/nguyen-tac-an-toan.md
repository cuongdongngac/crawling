---
title: "Safety rules when observing"
---

Finding keywords is a **passive, view-only** activity. The rules below protect you and keep the project in its research role.

## The "three don'ts"

1. **Don't interact** — don't buy, don't comment, don't message, don't follow the seller's account.
2. **Don't go looking for the source yourself** — don't verify in the real world.
3. **Don't act alone** — if anything seems unusual, report it to the provincial coordinator.

## When observing online

- Don't enter closed groups under a false identity.
- Don't collect the personal information of the person posting (name, phone number, address…).
- Limit searching on the account you use for communications; prefer listening through conversations, public sources, or a shared project account.
- If you suspect a production site or a large stash: **just take a screenshot, record it and report back** following the coordinator's instructions.

:::caution[Why be careful?]
Interacting with a seller's account can make the platform push more sales content toward you, or get you identified. The project only commits to **detecting and referring**, it does not ask you to verify anything yourself.
:::

## How your data is recorded

To assess and reward you based on **suggestion quality**, the system logs every trial scan you run: time, tool, keywords, number of posts, number of Vietnamese posts, number of new hashtags seen, cost, number of Excel exports. The system does **not store post content** from trial scans and does **not store your API key**.
