---
title: "Install the app on your phone"
---

The system is an **installable web app** (PWA): no need for Google Play or the App Store; install it from the browser and you get an icon on your home screen that opens full screen like a regular app.

## Android (Chrome, Samsung Internet, Edge)

1. Open the system's address in Chrome and **sign in**.
2. On the top bar, tap the blue **Install app** button → **Install**.
3. The **VPHA Quét** icon appears on your home screen.

:::tip[Don't see the "Install app" button?]
Open Chrome's ⋮ menu → **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**. If it is already installed, the button won't appear.
:::

## iPhone / iPad

1. Open the system's address in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (a square with an upward arrow) on the bottom bar.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
4. Open the **VPHA Quét** icon and **sign in once more** — an app installed on iPhone keeps its own sign-in session, separate from Safari.

## Notes

- The app opens straight to the **Trial scan** page.
- You need Internet to scan. If you lose connection, the scan already running on Apify **continues**; once you're back online, open the app to see the progress.
- The app updates itself when a new version is available, no reinstall needed.
