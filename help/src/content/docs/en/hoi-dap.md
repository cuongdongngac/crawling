### Does a trial scan cost money?
Yes, it is charged to **your** Apify account. The free plan has a monthly quota. Before scanning, the page shows an estimated maximum cost.

### What if I turn off my device midway?
The scan keeps running on Apify. Reopen the Trial scan page (same device, same browser) to see the progress. If you didn't save the key, you need to paste it again to continue.

### Why are the results all in English?
A generic keyword like "vape" returns global content. Use Vietnamese slang/hashtags and filter **Vietnamese only**.

### Does the system know what I scanned?
It logs activity (time, keywords, number of posts, cost) to assess based on suggestion quality. It does **not** store post content from trial scans and does **not** store the API key.

### I forgot my password / can't sign in?
Contact the provincial coordinator or an administrator to have it reset.

### On iPhone, why do I have to sign in again after installing?
An app installed from Safari keeps its own sign-in session. You only need to sign in once in the app.

### I see content that looks like a large stash?
Just take a screenshot, record it and report it to the coordinator. Don't verify it yourself. See [Safety rules](./bat-dau/nguyen-tac-an-toan).
