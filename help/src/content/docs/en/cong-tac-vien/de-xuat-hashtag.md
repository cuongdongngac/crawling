title: "Submit a hashtag suggestion"
---

The suggestion form is how you tell the Research team about a new hashtag/slang. **Form quality** (being approved, and you being the earliest suggester) is the main basis for crediting contributions — not the number of scans.

## Submit a form from trial scan results

1. In the **Co-occurring hashtags** tab, find a hashtag labeled **New**.
2. Click **Suggest**. A form appears, with the hashtag and platform pre-filled.

   ![Suggest button](/images/cong-tac-vien/de-xuat-hashtag-01-nut-de-xuat.png)
   <p class="img-caption">Figure 1: The Suggest button is only available for New hashtags. Note: generic hashtags like #fyp, #xuhuong also show as New but shouldn't be suggested</p>

3. **Select 1–10 supporting posts** (already sorted by engagement) — click **Open post** to review before selecting.
4. Score the **three criteria**: *Look at the image*, *Look at the text*, *Look at the transaction* (2/3 or more means a suspected case — see [The three-criteria rule](./ba-tieu-chi)).
5. Write the **Meaning / how this word is used** — for example: a slang name for a pod, often accompanied by a sale price…
6. Submit the form.

![Suggestion form](/images/cong-tac-vien/de-xuat-hashtag-02-phieu-mobile.png)
<p class="img-caption">Figure 2: Suggestion form: word meaning, three criteria (2/3 means a suspected case), supporting posts</p>

## Track the outcome

Go to the **Hashtag suggestions** item on the menu. Each form has a status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending review** | Research team hasn't looked at it yet |
| **Needs more info** | Read the reason, add supporting posts or explanation |
| **Approved** | The hashtag is added to the scan list |
| **Rejected** | With a reason |
| **Duplicate** | Someone already suggested it |

:::tip[A good form]
- Supporting posts are **Vietnamese** posts that clearly show how the word is used.
- Write the meaning briefly and specifically, saying clearly why it's worth tracking.
- Avoid generic hashtags (`#xuhuong`, `#fyp`…) — they co-occur with everything so they don't help detection.
:::
