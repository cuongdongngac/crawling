---
title: "The three-criteria rule"
---

So that everyone judges by the same yardstick, the project uses the **three criteria** rule when reviewing a post:

| Criterion | Look at | Example signs |
|---|---|---|
| **Look at the image** | Photos, videos | Products, devices, smoke |
| **Look at the text** | Content, hashtags | Evasive words, slang tied to the product |
| **Look at the transaction** | Content, public comments | Price, how to contact, delivery |

**Two or more of the three criteria means a _suspected case_.**

## Common forms of language variation

- **Renaming the product** — calling it by another name, a slang name.
- **Abbreviations, teencode**, spelling variations.
- **Emoji symbols** instead of words.
- **Disguising the purpose** — “clearance”, “passing on stuff”.
- **Moving to another platform** — “DM Zalo”, “join Telegram”.

:::tip[Calibration]
In the first week, everyone scores the same set of 50 simulated sample posts with answer keys to align interpretations; this set is re-run every 8–12 weeks because slang changes.
:::
