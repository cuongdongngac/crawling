title: "Review suggestions"
---

The **Hashtag suggestions** section for researchers lists every contributor's suggestion form, along with the province, evidence post, and three-criteria score.

## Decisions

Every decision **must record a reason**:

- **Approve** — the hashtag is added to the directory (adaptive group) and attached to the **hashtag group** you choose.
- **Needs additions** — sent back to the contributor with what needs to be added.
- **Reject** — with a reason.
- **Duplicate** — already in the directory or already suggested by someone earlier.

:::tip[Earliest suggester]
When several people suggest the same term, the system marks **the earliest suggester of this term**. The number of approved suggestions and being the earliest are the basis for recording monthly contributions (the **Contributor activity** section).
:::

## Tips when reviewing

- Retry the keyword with the platform search: if it appears on ~10 sales posts sharing the keyword, treat it as a real variant.
- Prioritize terms with **Vietnamese** evidence posts that meet at least 2/3 criteria.
- Assign the correct hashtag group so that analysis rounds stay comparable even when the list inside changes.
